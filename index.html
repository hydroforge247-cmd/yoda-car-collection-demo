02 · Safe bidding

Only real bids

Only people who can actually pay may bid. That protects the seller and every honest bidder.

  1. Create your bidder profileYour name, contact details, SA ID number and a password. One profile per person.
  2. Sign the bidder agreementYou agree that your bids are binding, and you sign on screen with your finger or mouse.
  3. Upload your IDA photo of the front and the back. Only YODA sees it, and it is used only to check who you are.
  4. We approve youWe check your details and approve your profile. Only approved bidders can bid.
  5. Bid anonymouslyOther bidders only see your bidder number, never your name.
  6. No last-second snipingA bid in the final 2 minutes adds 2 minutes, so everyone gets a fair chance to respond.
  7. Pay within 48 hoursThe winner pays by EFT within 48 hours. If not, the sale can be cancelled and the car offered to the next-highest bidder.

Bidder agreement

By signing, I agree with YODA Car Collection that:

  1. I am 18 or older, and the details and ID I give are true and my own.
  2. Every bid I place is a binding offer to buy the car for that amount. I cannot take a bid back once it is placed.
  3. If I am the highest bidder when the auction closes (and any reserve is met), I must buy the car. I will pay the full amount by EFT within 48 hours and arrange collection with YODA.
  4. I have read the car's description, including any faults listed, and I may view the car by appointment before I bid.
  5. If I do not pay in time, YODA may cancel the sale, offer the car to the next-highest bidder, close my bidder profile, and claim the reasonable costs and losses this causes, as the law allows.
  6. YODA may refuse a registration, and may suspend a bidder who breaks this agreement.
  7. My typed name and the signature I draw below are my electronic signature on this agreement.
  8. YODA uses my details and ID only to check who I am and to run the auction, as set out in the privacy notice.

Your privacy (POPIA)

YODA Car Collection collects your name, email, cellphone, SA ID number, a photo of your ID and your signed bidder agreement only to check that bidders are real people, to run the auction and to contact the winner.

Your details are seen only by YODA, never by other bidders, who see a bidder number instead. We do not sell or share them. If we don't approve your profile, we delete your ID photos. You can ask us to see, correct or delete your details at any time.
